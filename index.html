About The 1derful Festival

Every song on this lineup was a phenomenon. A cultural moment. A track that owned the radio, dominated the charts, and then — just like that — became the one song everyone knows by heart from an artist they couldn't name twice.

The 1derful Festival is a three-day beachfront celebration of exactly that magic. Held every August on the shores of Clearwater Beach, Florida, we bring together the artists behind the most beloved one-hit wonders of the 90s and 2000s for a weekend of music, nostalgia, and pure joy.

Whether you were singing along in a car, a school dance, or a living room — these songs were the soundtrack to a moment in your life. Come relive them with us.

Frequently Asked Questions

When and where is The 1derful Festival?

The 1derful Festival takes place August 14–16, 2026 on Clearwater Beach, Florida. The festival grounds are located at Pier 60 Park, right on the waterfront. Gates open at 3:00 PM each day.

How do I purchase tickets?

Tickets are available through our official ticketing partner. Single-day passes and three-day weekend passes are both available. We recommend purchasing in advance as the festival has sold out in previous years. Click the My Picks button in the navigation to save your favorite artists before buying.

Is the festival all ages?

Yes! The 1derful Festival is an all-ages event. Children 10 and under are admitted free with a paying adult. Please note that the beverage areas are restricted to guests 21 and older with valid ID.

What can I bring into the festival?

Guests are welcome to bring blankets, low-back chairs, sealed water bottles, and small bags. All bags are subject to search upon entry. Outside food and beverages, professional cameras, and glass containers are not permitted on the festival grounds.

Is there parking available?

Limited parking is available at the Pier 60 parking garage. We strongly encourage guests to use the free festival shuttle service running from Clearwater Mall, or to use rideshare services. Accessible parking is available on-site with valid permit.

What happens if it rains?

The 1derful Festival is a rain-or-shine event. In the event of severe weather or lightning, performances may be temporarily paused for guest safety. Refunds are not available for weather delays. We recommend bringing a poncho just in case!
